FluidR3 GM instrument samples by Frank Wen and contributors.
Rendered sample distribution: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
Source files: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/gh-pages/FluidR3_GM

Selected samples are extracted unchanged from the acoustic_grand_piano,
orchestral_harp, string_ensemble_1 and contrabass MP3 soundfont distributions.
Runtime playback varies pitch, gain, note length and arrangement.
Original game compositions are distinct from the soundfont recordings.
