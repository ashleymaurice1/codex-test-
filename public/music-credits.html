Music credits

Midnight Ghost Arcade uses these finished instrumental recordings from the game soundtrack:

Home page

  • “Creepy Hallow” — Alexander Nakarada
  • “Witch Waltz” — Kevin MacLeod

Jumping game

“Del Rio Bravo” — Kevin MacLeod. A Mexican-inspired instrumental selected for the Día de los Muertos mood; not a traditional ceremonial recording.

Rhythm game

  • “Ghostlight Parade” — original theatrical Halloween march
  • “Midnight Spell” — original witchy house instrumental
  • “Lantern Lullaby” — original eerie lullaby
  • “Velvet Cathedral” — original gothic instrumental
  • “Night in the Castle” — Kevin MacLeod

The four originals were arranged for Midnight Ghost Arcade and rendered with sampled virtual instruments from GeneralUser GS by S. Christian Collins. They use original melodies, without samples or melodies from the reference film songs. Instrument library license permits private and commercial music creation.

Source for the other recordings: SoundSafari CC0-1.0-Music / FreePD collection. Distributed under CC0 1.0; license text.

The game copies use gentle loudness normalization and MP3 re-encoding. Rhythm charts use original melody cue times or measured audible attacks; the music itself is played as a full recording rather than synthesized note loops.
