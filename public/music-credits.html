Music credits

Midnight Zombies uses these finished instrumental recordings from the FreePD public-domain collection:

  • “Witch Waltz” — Kevin MacLeod
  • “Magic in the Garden” — Rafael Krux
  • “Fright Night Twist” — Bryan Teoh
  • “Creepy Comedy” — Rafael Krux
  • “Night in the Castle” — Kevin MacLeod

Source: SoundSafari CC0-1.0-Music / FreePD collection. Distributed under CC0 1.0; license text.

The game copies use gentle loudness normalization and MP3 re-encoding. Rhythm charts use detected beat times from each recording; the music itself is played as a full recording rather than synthesized note loops.
