## Project description

Midnight Ghost Arcade is a creepy-cute Halloween arcade built with OpenAI, combining jumping, maze exploration, and multiplayer tap racing. Play as Mach the ghost falcon, Slink the ghost serpent, Ember the ghost wolf, or Misty the tiny ghost bat in an enchanted bioluminescent woodland. Twelve one-tap challenge types keep each run varied. Fast, correct taps move you forward; wrong taps make your ghost flicker out for a moment. Earn candy boosts and mischievous items, and race as Mach the falcon, Slink the serpent, Ember the wolf, or Misty the bat.

Start with the one-button Moonlit Leap course, then explore Ghostlight Maze, or play original tap races against personality-driven rivals. After a win, increase the difficulty of that style or advance and switch styles. Share a four-letter room code with friends for a live multiplayer tap race on their own phones. The server keeps challenges, positions, items, and the winner synchronized. The original tap-race campaign progresses through four unlockable courses: Spiral-Hill Graveyard, Crooked Pumpkin Patch, Leaning Haunted House, and Monster Bash Bell Tower. The first level takes only five easy taps; later levels introduce more challenge types, faster timers, and tougher rivals. Completed solo challenges advance automatically after a short celebration. Choose your ghost before racing. Earn medals, coins, and cosmetic rewards saved in your browser. Solo starts with three hearts; buy spare lives with earned coins or receive one automatically every 500 score points. A compact live leaderboard stays visible during races. Nicknames are entered once across styles; winners make one gentle color-trailing swirl and settle proudly while other podium characters stay still.

The original art features translucent, flowing ghost creatures, inspired by the creator's own Mach video. Glowing blue moonlight and warm amber lanterns light a bioluminescent woodland with one crooked haunted house and a full moon. The creator’s original soundtrack continues smoothly with crossfaded loops, alongside varied Halloween encouragement. No login or installation is required.

## Hosting notes for a demo

The Render free instance can take about a minute to wake after inactivity. Rooms are in memory and are lost on a server restart; personal rewards and level progress remain in that browser. Open the game before demonstrating it. Global weekly team standings are not implemented; the game shows personal team points.

The arcade offers a 20-game Journey through Jump, Melody, Maze and Tap Race, plus standalone Quick Play. Jump has Classic, Marathon and Endless modes; the mazes have escape loops and smooth buffered turns. Tap Race has three difficulty settings, shuffled challenge bags and changing targets. English/Spanish selection, personal high scores, player reset, phone touch controls and ready countdowns are included. Mach, Slink, Ember and Misty retain their distinct designs and gentle expressions.

Melody song 1 is the creator-supplied “La Mesa Puesta Para Ti,” played in full for about 90 seconds. Its three audio-derived charts contain 36, 73 and 161 notes, with two, three and four arrow lanes. Hardware output timestamps help align gameplay with audible music when supported. Other tracks retain their original arrangements and FreePD credits; the supplied song is credited separately. Listen/Pause previews and saved volume preferences are included. The public Story image and download page are available at `/story.html`.

Current soundtrack update: Four Strange Footsteps and Halloween Belongs to Our Crew are the selected creator-supplied recordings throughout the arcade. The complete 144.8-second recording has three audio-derived difficulty charts with 80, 159 and 317 notes. Previous recordings are removed.
